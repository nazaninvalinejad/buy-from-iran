Who we are

A platform built on
trust, not just listings

Buy From Iran (BFI) brings international buyers face-to-face with verified manufacturers, exporters, and wholesalers from across Iran — cutting through the noise of unreliable middlemen and unverifiable claims.

We built BFI because we saw the same problem repeat itself: buyers had no reliable way to distinguish a capable, export-ready factory from a trader with a borrowed catalogue. Every listing on our platform reflects a real, audited business — not just a name on a page.

How it started

Three people. One clear problem to solve.

BFI was founded in 2020 by a team with more than 20 years of combined experience in international supply chains, market development, and the construction industry. That experience taught us something simple but important: buyers don't just need a list of suppliers — they need a way to know which ones they can actually trust.

We started with just three people. Within our first year, that number had grown to seventeen — a team united by one goal: introducing the quality and diversity of Iranian-made products to buyers around the world, and making the process of sourcing from Iran as straightforward as sourcing from anywhere else.

What drives us

Iran's manufacturers deserve a
global audience

Iran is home to a vast and often underrepresented base of manufacturers — from cement and bitumen producers to saffron growers, carpet weavers, and industrial equipment makers. Much of it never reaches international markets, not because the quality isn't there, but because the pathway to reach global buyers has historically been fragmented, informal, or simply invisible.

Our mission is to close that gap. We do this by combining industry expertise with a rigorous approach to supplier verification, so that every listing on our platform reflects a real, capable, export-ready business.

How we work

Verification first.
Always.

Every supplier on Buy From Iran goes through a structured verification process before they appear on the platform. Buyers can browse, compare, quote, and ship — with support at every step.

1
Documentation review
We review business registration, export licences, and relevant certifications before any supplier is listed. If something is missing, we don't list until it's provided.
2
Capacity & track record evaluation
We assess production capacity, past export history, and quality certifications such as ISO, SGS, and product-specific standards relevant to each category.
3
Ongoing buyer ratings
After each transaction, buyers rate their experience. Suppliers whose performance slips are reviewed and, if necessary, suspended — keeping the network's quality high.
4
Logistics & inspection support
Buyers can coordinate pre-shipment inspection, payment terms, and port-to-port logistics through BFI — from first contact to a delivered order.
Where we're headed

Whether you're sourcing construction materials, agricultural products, textiles, or industrial equipment, our team is here to help you find the right partner in Iran — and make sure the partnership works.

Our story

From three people to
a global platform

2020
BFI is founded

Three industry veterans — with backgrounds in construction materials, export logistics, and market development — launch Buy From Iran with a simple mission: make Iranian manufacturing discoverable and trustworthy for global buyers.

2020 — Year one
Team grows to 17

Within the first twelve months, BFI's team expands from 3 to 17 specialists spanning supplier verification, logistics coordination, customer support, and platform development. The first wave of verified suppliers goes live.

2021 – 2022
Verification framework launched

BFI introduces its structured supplier verification process — covering documentation, export history, capacity, and quality certifications — making it the most rigorous vetting standard in the Iran B2B sourcing space.

2023 – 2024
120+ buyer countries

Buyers from over 120 countries have now used BFI to source Iranian products — from construction materials and petrochemicals to agricultural goods and handicrafts. Port-to-port logistics support is added to the platform.

Today
9,000+ verified suppliers and growing

BFI now lists over 9,000 verified Iranian suppliers across dozens of industries, with more than 50,000 products available. We remain committed to making every connection faster, safer, and more transparent than the last.
