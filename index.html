BFI Is Helping You Connect With The Best Wholesale Material Suppliers From Iran

BuyFromIran (BFI) welcomes buyers from all over the world to its comprehensive marketplace — a wholesale hub where buyers can connect directly with trusted suppliers, manufacturers, and wholesalers from across Iran.

BFI was founded in 2020 by a team with over 20 years of experience in international supply chains, market development, and the construction industry. The desire to share the team's professional expertise in construction materials and industrial products, along with the ambition to introduce the quality and diversity of products manufactured in Iran to global markets, is the common vision that brought this team together.

We started BuyFromIran with just 3 members, and before completing our first year of operation, we had grown into a team of 17 professionals dedicated to helping international buyers source high-quality products from reliable Iranian manufacturers and suppliers.

Today, BuyFromIran serves as a trusted platform that bridges the gap between global buyers and Iranian producers, making it easier to discover quality products, competitive prices, and dependable business partners from Iran.
