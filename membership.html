Questions

Frequently asked

Can I switch plans at any time?
Yes. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your membership at any time from your account dashboard. Upgrades take effect immediately; downgrades apply at the end of your current billing cycle.
Is there a free trial for the Pro plan?
Yes. Pro Buyer comes with a 14-day free trial — no credit card required. If you decide Pro isn't right for you, you'll automatically roll back to the free Starter plan at the end of the trial.
How does "verified supplier" status work?
Every supplier on Buy From Iran goes through our three-stage verification: business licence check, export history review, and a capacity assessment by our in-country team. Verified badges are reviewed annually and can be revoked if standards are not maintained.
What currencies and payment methods do you accept?
Membership fees are billed in USD. We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), as well as SEPA bank transfer for annual Enterprise contracts. Invoicing is available for annual plans.
Does my membership cover the cost of goods or shipping?
No. Membership gives you access to our platform, tools, and support. All commercial transactions — goods pricing, payment, and shipping — are negotiated directly between you and the supplier. We provide guidance and facilitation, not financial intermediation.
What is the difference between a Supplier and a Buyer membership?
The plans shown here are for buyers (importers, procurement teams, trading companies). If you are an Iranian manufacturer or exporter looking to list your products, please visit our supplier registration page or contact us — supplier listing plans are separate and structured around visibility and lead volume.
